a short story on me

My journey so far

  1. December 2025, Starting from zero: I started graphic design as a complete beginner, kept learning, and took whatever projects I could get as a starter. Discord servers gave me my first projects. The clients were unprofessional, demanding high-quality work for low pay, and some didn't even pay after delivery and ghosted me.
  2. January 2026, First portfolio, first paychecks: I started making my first-ever portfolio and cold-mailing companies for possible commissions. I landed a few commissions and got paid for them. I also made my first-ever brand identity in these months.
  3. February 2026, My first 'job': I met Generation Uprising through a Discord server and began designing social media carousels for their Instagram.
  4. March 2026, A project that mattered: My first real project that mattered. I made a brand identity, basic motion graphics, business cards and basic packaging design for a family business.
  5. March to August 2026, Learning, always: I kept learning more and more about this field, kept doing commissions, and kept working for small brands.
  6. September 2026, Simira Diagnostics (current): I started searching for proper jobs online and met Simira Diagnostics, a diagnostic lab that's also dipping its toes into social media. I joined their marketing wing as a video editor.
  7. The next chapter: I'm still eager to learn and advance in this field. I don't know what's planned for me in the future, and that thought both scares and amazes me.
